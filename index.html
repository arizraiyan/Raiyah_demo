How a piece is made

Four weeks, four hands

These steps run in order. Nothing moves forward until the previous stage is signed off by the karigar who did it.

Week 01

Fabric & cut

We buy in short lengths from mills in Surat and Bhuj, wash-test every roll for colour bleed, then cut to your size chart.

Week 02

Frame & embroider

Panels go onto the aari frame. Zari, dabka and mirror work are done here — by hand, one motif at a time.

Week 03

Stitch & line

Embroidered panels are joined, lined in soft cotton, and every edge is rolled rather than overlocked.

Week 04

Finish & check

Pressed, measured against the original spec, photographed, and packed in a cotton bag with the karigar's name inside.
